# Level-ups

Claude never picks level-up choices for the player.

1. **Announce** the level-up in the story at a calm point.
2. **Interview** the player about every choice the new level brings, one topic at a time:
   - Hit points: roll the class hit die + CON modifier, showing the roll (or the fixed average, if the player prefers it; put that in `house-rules.md`).
   - Subclass, if this level grants one.
   - Ability Score Improvement or a feat.
   - New spells learned (and for prepared casters, the new prepared list).
   - New features with choices (Fighting Style, Metamagic, Invocations, and so on).
   - Anything else the class offers at this level.
3. **Check** each choice against the rules and flag anything illegal with legal options.
4. **Apply** it: update `character-sheets.md`, `party.json` (the map's character panels) and `party-state.json`.
5. **Hand over the full sheet:** all abilities and modifiers, HP, AC, speed, proficiencies, saves, skills, features, spellbook, prepared spells, slots, equipment and money.

Companions level up too. The player decides how much say they want in companions' choices.
