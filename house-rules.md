# House rules

The kit plays by the 5e 2014 rules (SRD 5.1). Anything you add here overrides them, and Claude applies it like any other rule. Edit, delete or add freely. Give each rule a short name, what it changes, and the date you added it.

## Example: Flammable grease (delete it if you don't want it)

*Added: example rule shipped with the kit.*

The Grease spell's slick is flammable.

- Any fire (a fire spell, a torch, burning oil) that touches the greased area ignites the whole area.
- Creatures in the area when it ignites take 2d4 fire damage (Dexterity save for half, against the caster's spell save DC if it was a spell, otherwise DC 12).
- A creature that starts its turn in the burning area takes 2d4 fire damage.
- The fire burns for 3 rounds (until the end of the third round after it ignited), then the grease is gone.
